Lercher Lab

Inflammatory Signal Integration and Inflammatory Memory

Our Approach

We study how immune cells integrate diverse inflammatory signals over time and translate them into lasting functional states. By combining systems-level profiling with functional immunology, we aim to uncover the molecular logic of inflammatory memory — and how it shapes health and disease.

Learn more about our research

Research

The Problem

Immune cells are constantly exposed to a barrage of inflammatory signals — cytokines, microbial products, metabolic cues — often simultaneously and repeatedly over time. Yet we still understand very little about how cells integrate these combinatorial signals into coherent decisions, or how prior inflammatory encounters leave lasting imprints that reshape future responses. This "inflammatory memory" may underlie both protective immunity and maladaptive chronic disease, but the molecular rules governing it remain largely unknown.

The Approach

We combine multi-omic profiling, functional perturbation, and quantitative modeling to dissect how immune cells process combinations of inflammatory signals. Using in vitro and in vivo systems, we track how signal integration shapes cell states — and persists as memory — to identify the molecular circuits that could be targeted therapeutically.
